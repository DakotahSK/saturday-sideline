# Saturday Sideline idea list

Ideas Dakotah wants to keep for later. Nothing here is built yet. Ask him which one to do before starting any of them.

## Big features (suggested 2026-11-11)

1. **Real player rosters.** A full roster for every team (names, positions, class years, ratings). Unlocks player development, a depth chart, season stats and league leaders, All-Conference teams, award races based on real numbers, and seniors graduating. Biggest upgrade; plan it carefully so the save keeps working and stays under the cloud size limits (derive rosters from seeds where possible).
2. **Weekly game plan.** A short prep screen before each game: scout the opponent's tendencies, pick a focus (stop the run, protect the QB, attack a weak corner) and set practice intensity with injury risk.
3. **Coaching career mode.** Hire coordinators with styles and ratings who can get poached, field offers from bigger programs (stay or leave Averyville), coaching tree, legacy score, Hall of Fame.
4. **Deeper recruiting.** Home visits, campus visit weekends, rival schools competing for the same recruit, commitment flips, a live Signing Day show.
5. **League with friends.** Use the artifact's shared data so a friend can coach another team; the league advances on a schedule and head-to-head games count.
6. **History hall.** A page per program: title banners, all-time leaders, timeline of great seasons, coaching history, greatest games.
7. **Highlights.** Auto-save big plays, a weekly top 10 plays reel, a season-ending package for the champion.
8. **Commissioner tools.** Change playoff size, realign conferences, add expansion teams, set parity strength and schedule length from inside the game.

Pick if only one: real player rosters.

## Simple add-ons (suggested 2026-11-11, he called these "pretty simple")

Radio broadcast mode (browser voices read the play-by-play), build your stadium with booster money, uniform designer and throwback week, the Sunday paper front page, broadcast booth personalities who remember history, locker room chemistry, eras every ten seasons, replay classic games from league history.

## Outside-the-box ideas inspired by other games (suggested 2026-11-11)

1. **Playbook deckbuilder** (Slay the Spire, Balatro). Plays are cards. Add new ones each offseason from coaching clinics, upgrade favorites, build combos (play-action gets stronger after the same look twice). Opponents learn tendencies, so an overused card loses power until you set it up again.
2. **Bloodlines** (Crusader Kings). Players and coaches have families. A star's son shows up as a recruit about 18 seasons later and his father's school wants him. Former players become assistants, then head coaches running the schemes they played in. Family rivalries across programs.
3. **Nemesis coaches** (Shadow of Mordor). Rival coaches remember you. Blow one out and he returns with a scheme built to beat yours and talks about it all week. Beat him enough and he is fired or humbled; lose and he rises to a bigger job. Each has a profile: record against you, grudges, signature moments.
4. **Legends and curses** (Dwarf Fortress). The league names its own history ("The Whiteout", a curse after a last-second loss that lasts until broken). Names show up in broadcasts, storylines and a league museum.
5. **Your week as a calendar** (Persona). A few time slots each week: film study, player one-on-ones, recruiting visits, church community events, booster time. Bonds with players unlock clutch moments. You cannot do everything.
6. **The town grows with you** (Stardew Valley, SimCity). Averyville as a small town map that grows with winning (diner, billboard, crowds) and empties during losing stretches. Townspeople with stories and favors.
7. **Fog of war scouting** (XCOM). Opponent ratings and tendencies are hidden until scouted, which costs calendar time. Unscouted teams are question marks.
8. **The bus ride** (Oregon Trail). Road trips as short event chains (breakdowns, storms, homesick players, a host church dinner). Choices affect rest and focus at kickoff.

Claude's top three from this list: nemesis coaches, legends and curses, bloodlines (they all build on the league history already in the save).
